## 👥 KeenKeeper — Keep Your Friendships Alive

## Project Overview : 
**KeenKeeper** is a personal relationship management (PRM) platform designed to help users intentionally maintain and nurture their friendships. It tracks communication intervals to ensure meaningful connections don't fade away.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **📦 Centralized Dashboard**
  An at-a-glance hub showing interaction metrics and a friend grid with color-coded status badges (e.g., *On Track*, *Overdue*).

* **⏳ Custom Contact Cadence**
  Set individual communication goals (e.g., *Connect every 30 days*) and track the exact days elapsed since your last contact.

* **⚡ Quick Check-In Actions**
  One-click shortcuts (**Call**, **Text**, **Video**) directly on friend profiles to make reaching out seamless.

* **📅 Interaction Timeline**
  A chronological, filterable history log tracking all past social activities (Meetups, Calls, Texts) by date.

* **📊 Friendship Analytics**
  Visual data charts breaking down your interaction types to give you insights into your personal socializing habits.
